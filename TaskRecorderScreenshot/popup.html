D365FO TR Plus Screenshots

Open this popup while on a Dynamics tab.
  1. Site access must be On all sites (required for tab screenshots).
  2. Chrome Incognito: Allow in Incognito, quit Chrome, open a new Incognito window.
  3. Task recorder → Create recording → Capture screenshots = Yes → Start.
  4. Keep the F&O tab in front while recording; background tabs can’t be captured.
  5. Screenshots appear in the Word export, not as step thumbnails.
